      How returns are counted

      A returned parcel costs you the courier charge both ways, the packaging, any stock that can't be resold, and the ad money you spent to get that order. Most sellers only count the first one.

      Ads Manager ROAS vs real ROAS

      Ads Manager counts every order placed. Real ROAS only counts orders that were delivered and paid. With 20% returns, a "5x" campaign is really 4x.
