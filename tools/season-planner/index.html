        Why start this early?

        Meta needs a few days to learn who buys. Brands that test before the rush can scale winners when demand peaks, instead of learning during the most expensive days.

        Courier cut-offs

        Before Eid, couriers get flooded and many people travel home. Orders outside Dhaka placed in the last few days often arrive late, and late parcels get returned.

        Where the dates come from

        Eid dates are calculated with the Islamic (Umm al-Qura) calendar. In Bangladesh, Eid is usually the same day or one day later, depending on the moon. We plan around the earlier date, to be safe.
